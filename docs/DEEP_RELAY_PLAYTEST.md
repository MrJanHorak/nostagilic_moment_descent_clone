# Deep Relay human playtest

Choose **Select Level → Deep Relay** in a normal Chrome session. M1 does not need another acceptance run. M3 remains open until this larger mission has been played and any material issues fixed. The automated checks separate traversal from combat, so this combined playthrough matters.

## Route

1. Start in **Blue Docking Bay** with 100 shield, seventy pulse rounds, five drones and two inactive relays. You should have time to get oriented behind cover before entering a doorway.
2. Explore the lower loop: **Amber Ore Works → Green Coolant Loop → Blue Lift Junction → Docking Bay**. The HUD names your current room and the next passage. Clear the first fighter and scout; wall edges should provide usable cover.
3. From the junction, visit **Amber Supply Cache** through the side passage if you need supplies. Its narrow corridor contains ammo and a repair, offset to either side along its length. Full inventory leaves pickups available.
4. Find the cyan **Lift power relay** in the junction. Press **R** when its prompt appears. Its beacon turns green and the amber ceiling hatch opens. Fly through into the taller **Green Upper Lift**. Another repair is near its lower side.
5. Clear the upper fighter encounters and enter **Blue Relay Control** through the side passage. Activate the **Extraction relay** with R to open the last door.
6. Enter **Green Extraction**, clear its scout and fly into the ring near the far wall. The ring is amber until all five drones and both relays are complete, then green. Completion should happen once.
7. Replay: verify spawn, full shield, seventy rounds, five drones, two locked gates and zero relay/defeat progress. Briefly pause and resume during a run to check that pointer capture and the objective chain still feel right.

W/S thrust forward/back, A/D strafe, Space/Shift move vertically, Q/E roll, mouse/arrows aim, click/F fire and Esc pauses. Movement follows ship orientation, including roll. The route hints name world ceiling/floor connections; they are not a screen-relative compass.

## Record after playing

- Browser/GPU and approximate completion time.
- Any room or doorway where guidance was confusing; whether you found both relays and extraction without reading this route.
- Shield and ammo remaining; any unavoidable damage, crowding at doors or long periods searching for a drone.
- Whether pickups were visible and useful, and whether the quiet spawn allowed enough preparation.
- Any stuck collision, enemy crossing a closed gate, incomplete objective, repeated reward, broken replay or pause/capture problem.
