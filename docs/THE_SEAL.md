# Mission 05: The Seal

The first mission of Act 2. Kestrel goes back into the Asterion mine without a contract to evacuate the miners trapped below FOREMAN's seal. It follows Asterion Deepworks in the campaign; **NEXT MISSION** after Deepworks leads here.

- **File:** `src/js/core/levels/documents/the-seal.v14.json`, produced by `node scripts/build-the-seal.mjs`. Edit the script and re-run it rather than editing the JSON by hand; the script validates before writing.
- **Size:** 26 named spaces on two levels 40 units apart, 28 passages, three independent loops, two vertical shafts and a secret maintenance drop between levels.
- **Format:** 14, for room names in the pilot's guide, with the full story block.

## Objectives

1. Shoot open the striped dock door.
2. Activate **Main shaft power** in the Pump hall (a bomber guards it). This drops the force field over the main shaft in the Shaft gallery.
3. Descend to the **Seal antechamber** and destroy both Consortium Asset Protection fighters (required).
4. Activate **Seal release** in Seal control. It opens the seal bulkhead to the lower works and the gate back from the Old smelter.
5. Destroy the **Asset Protection destroyer** in Evac lift control (required) and activate **Evacuation lift power**.
6. The Consortium override floods the works: **75 seconds** to climb the evac lift shaft, cross the Pump hall and Shaft gallery, and reach the exit ring in the Kestrel dock.

Optional: four miners (Hana Okafor and Luis Ferro in the Lower dorms, Pavel Ostrov in the Flood sump, Kit Brandt in the Old smelter) and four logs. Miners count only if you reach the exit. Grab them before powering the lift, or on a fast run during the countdown.

## Story beats

Vey frames the mission as a vote: Kestrel chooses the miners over the money. FOREMAN, now openly cooperative, holds the shaft field for Two and admits that releasing the seal "lets them out, and lets them in". The logs give a roll call from the dorms, FOREMAN's seal log (day 1 to "DAY 17: TWO RETURNED"), Asset Protection's standing orders (flood the works if the lift powers up, treat Kestrel-2 as hostile) and a note scratched by Inés, who was rescued in Deepworks. Powering the lift triggers the Consortium flood: FOREMAN cannot stop it, only warn you.
