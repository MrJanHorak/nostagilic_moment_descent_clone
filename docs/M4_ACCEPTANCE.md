# M4 functional acceptance

On October 19, 2026 the owner confirmed: “The level editor works, and I can play through the levels.” This closes M4's functional author/play/export milestone, following the automated author/export/reload/complete/return and Undo coverage. The owner also requested a more intuitive editor; that presentation work continues after acceptance.

Accepted capabilities include connected room/corridor authoring, loops, relay doors, ramp/shaft geometry, item placement, solid preview, real-runtime playtest/return, portable JSON and draft recovery. Acceptance does not make arbitrary geometry, texture uploads, maximum-world hardware performance or a finished campaign part of M4.

M1 and M3 remain accepted. M2's hosted CI gate is owner-confirmed; its hardware performance gate remains open. New ship presentation, music and campaign work require their own review and are described in [the player experience follow-up](PLAYER_EXPERIENCE.md).
